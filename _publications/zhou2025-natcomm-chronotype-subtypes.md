Chronotype is shaped by the complex interplay of endogenous and exogenous factors. Despite its multifaceted nature, prior research has treated chronotype as a monolithic trait, risking overlooking substantial heterogeneity in neural and behavioral fingerprints.

We developed a supervised pattern-learning framework integrating three complementary brain-imaging modalities with deep behavioral and health profiling from 27,030 UK Biobank participants. We identified **five distinct, biologically valid chronotype subtypes**, each demonstrating unique patterns across brain, behavioral, and health profiles. External validation in 10,550 US children from the ABCD Study cohort revealed reversed age distributions and replicated sex-associated brain-behavioral patterns.

**Key contributions:**
- First unsupervised discovery of latent chronotype subtypes from multimodal brain data
- Five distinct subtypes with unique brain, behavioral, and health signatures
- External replication in an independent pediatric cohort (ABCD Study)
