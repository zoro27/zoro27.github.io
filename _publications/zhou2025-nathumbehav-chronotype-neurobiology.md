Although individual differences in chronotype yield varying health and behavioral consequences, their neurobiological underpinnings remain elusive.

We conducted a pattern-learning analysis with **three brain-imaging modalities** (grey matter volume, white-matter integrity, and functional connectivity) and capitalized on **976 phenotypes** in **27,030 UK Biobank participants**. The resulting multilevel analysis reveals convergence on the **basal ganglia, limbic system, hippocampus, and cerebellum**, providing the first comprehensive mapping of chronotype onto the human brain's structural and functional architecture.

**Key contributions:**
- First multimodal population neuroimaging study of chronotype
- Convergence on basal ganglia, limbic system, hippocampus, and cerebellum
- Large-scale analysis across 976 phenotypes and three imaging modalities
